AI in Mental Health

I design and evaluate LLM agents for suicidal ideation detection and study the safety of mental health support chatbots.

DragSI. I designed the agent architecture, prompts, and lexicon integration, and led evaluation using 9,903 Weibo posts. The framework manuscript is under review.

03 AI FOR CONSTRUCTION SAFETY

AI for Construction Safety

I investigate how vision-language models can support construction-site safety analysis, using video data and comparative experiments across seven models.

Inside the research +

Construction-site video analysis. I collected TikTok video data through the TikTok API and designed LLM prompts and video-clip experiments for construction-site analysis.

Model comparison and safety research. I compared seven commercial and open-source models, reviewed construction-safety literature, and drafted a journal manuscript outline. Manuscript development is ongoing.
